The taxi hummed to life in the quiet **Katsabas** neighborhood, pulling away from a side street near the quaint *local bakery "Artozacharoplasteio"* as the first light painted the sky. It swiftly navigated smaller roads, then merged onto the bustling **Leoforos Knossou**, where morning commuters began their day.

A decisive turn at the major **intersection of Leoforos Knossou and Ethnikis Antistaseos Avenue** shifted the journey westward. The city lights blurred as the cab sped along **Leoforos Ikarou**, offering glimpses of the port in the distance. Finally, a sweeping curve onto **Andrea Papandreou Street** led into the coastal district of **Ammoudara**, concluding the ride near the iconic *Ammoudara Beach*, the salty air a refreshing end.
