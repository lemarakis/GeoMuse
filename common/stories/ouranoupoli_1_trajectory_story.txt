The taxi, a silent observer, pulled away from a tranquil street off **Odyssea Eliti** in **Thermi**. Morning sun glinted off suburban homes as we accelerated, joining the broader flow of **Leoforos Georgikis Scholis**.

Past the verdant campus of the **American Farm School**, the urban sprawl thickened. The driver skillfully navigated, turning sharply onto **Ethnikis Antistaseos**. The aroma of fresh bougatsa wafted from a corner bakery as we delved deeper into **Kalamaria**. The journey culminated at a lively intersection near **Plateia Skra**, where the city's vibrant pulse throbbed, signaling our arrival.
