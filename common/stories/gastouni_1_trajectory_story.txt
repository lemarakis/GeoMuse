The taxi idled, morning sun dappling through olive trees as it pulled away from the charming *Kourtaki Guesthouse* (21.26, 37.85). We drifted onto *Eparchiaki Odos Argos-Tripolis*, a ribbon of asphalt cutting through the Argolic plain. The ancient *Sanctuary of Heraion* flashed by as the driver settled into a steady rhythm. Houses thinned, replaced by citrus groves. Approaching the picturesque village of *Kefalari*, we made a sharp left onto *Odos Agiou Georgiou*, a narrower lane winding past the inviting *Taverna To Perivoli*. The journey ended at a secluded hillside, the engine sighing to a halt, a new adventure beginning.
