The taxi idled by the sun-kissed sands of Karteros Beach, the morning light glinting off its chrome. Picking up a fare, it smoothly pulled onto **Leoforos Ikarou**, leaving the tranquil coast behind. We zipped past **Heraklion Airport**, the drone of planes fading as we entered the bustling **Nea Alikarnassos** neighborhood.

The journey continued west, navigating the lively **Leoforos 62 Martyron** through Heraklion's outskirts, the city's energy palpable. Soon, we were cruising along **Leoforos Andrea Papandreou**, the azure Aegean reappearing as we entered the vibrant resort area of **Ammoudara**. A quick turn near the **Candia Maris Resort** led us through a labyrinth of side streets before settling on a quieter road, the trip concluding with the gentle hum of the engine.
