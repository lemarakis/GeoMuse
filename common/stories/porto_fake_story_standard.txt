The screen pulses with a vibrant, neon glow – the **Francisco Sá Carneiro Airport (OPO)** heatmap, your energetic arrival point in Porto. From here, your taxi adventure begins, leaving the airport's hum.

**EXT. PORTO STREETS - DAY**

Imagine sweeping down **Avenida da Boavista**, a modern artery, into the city's heart. Soon, the grand **Aliados Avenue** greets you, a majestic welcome. Your driver drops you, and your cinematic journey unfolds.

First, climb the iconic **Clérigos Tower** for breathtaking city views. Nearby, lose yourself in the enchanting **Livraria Lello**, its staircase a masterpiece. Don't miss the stunning azulejo tiles inside **São Bento Railway Station**. Wander to the ancient **Porto Cathedral**, then descend into the lively **Ribeira District**, where colorful houses meet the Douro River.

Cross the magnificent **Dom Luís I Bridge** to Vila Nova de Gaia, where the **Port Wine Cellars** await – a tasting is essential! Back in Porto, explore the buzzing **Mercado do Bolhão** for local delicacies or step back in time at the opulent **Majestic Café**. Admire the neoclassical splendor of **Palácio da Bolsa** and the striking architecture of **Casa da Música**. For retail therapy, stroll down pedestrian **Rua de Santa Catarina**. Finally, a taxi whisks you to **Foz do Douro** for a serene coastal walk. Every drop-off, a new scene in your unforgettable Porto story.
