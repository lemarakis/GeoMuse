The taxi idled by Ribeira’s ancient stones, overlooking the Douro. With a surge, it climbed steep Rua de São João, past flickering lamplight. We sped north through Cedofeita, art galleries blurring, then onto Avenida da Boavista, Casa da Música a futuristic marvel. A long stretch on the Circunvalação dissolved into a confused U-turn near Hospital de São João, a momentary detour.

Finally, a sharp turn off the highway, winding down Rua de Campo Alegre. The car paused briefly by the serene Jardins do Palácio de Cristal, before navigating the bustling streets. Our journey ended near the iconic Majestic Café on Rua de Santa Catarina, a silent drop-off in the heart of Porto.
