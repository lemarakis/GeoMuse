The yellow taxi hummed to life in Gerakas, pulling away from a side street near a bustling **My market**. We smoothly joined **Leoforos Marathonos**, the eastern suburbs gradually giving way to the urban sprawl of Agia Paraskevi. The city's rhythm deepened as we turned onto **Leoforos Mesogeion**, a blur of shops and cafes.

Soon, the grand **Kifissias Avenue** stretched ahead. Chalandri's vibrant energy faded into the upscale calm of Kifissia, its leafy streets hinting at old-world charm. Past the stately residences of Ekali, the road narrowed. Finally, we arrived at a quiet driveway in Varibobi, the journey ending amidst the scent of pine, a tranquil escape from Athens' embrace.
