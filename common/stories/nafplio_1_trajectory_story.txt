The taxi, a silent silver streak, departed from a tranquil side street in **Agios Konstantinos**. It smoothly curved north-east, then quickly merged onto the roaring **E75 Motorway**, the Athens-Thessaloniki National Road, with the shimmering Malian Gulf unfolding to our right.

After a rapid ascent, we veered off, executing a sharp turn onto the older, more scenic **P.E.O. Athinon-Lamias**, now heading south-west. The landscape shifted as we approached **Kamena Vourla**. Navigating past inviting local tavernas, the air grew thick with a distinctive mineral scent. Our journey culminated at the renowned **Thermal Springs of Kamena Vourla**, the final stop announced by the therapeutic steam rising gently into the afternoon sky.
