The taxi purred to life by **Karteros Beach**, the morning sun glinting off the Aegean. We pulled away from a quiet side street near the "Karteros Apartments," weaving through sleepy local lanes before joining the main **Palaia Ethniki Odos**.

The coastal road unspooled eastward, a ribbon of asphalt through **Kokkini Hani**'s bustling resorts. We passed the turn-off for the **Cretaquarium** in **Gournes**, its distinctive architecture a fleeting blur. The journey continued, the scent of salt and oleander filling the air. Finally, the taxi eased to a stop outside the "Gouves Bay Hotel," our destination nestled by the golden sands of **Kato Gouves**.
